## How to apply

Send a commercial-license request to the Technology Transfer Center of Southern University of Science and Technology at [ttc@sustech.edu.cn](mailto:ttc@sustech.edu.cn) with the subject:

`MetaSpici Commercial License Request - [Organization Name]`

Include:

1. the applicant's full legal name, registration jurisdiction, address, and website;
2. the name, title, and contact details of an authorized representative;
3. a clear description of the intended use, users, deployment model, and workflow;
4. whether MetaSpici will be modified, embedded, redistributed, hosted, or exposed through an API;
5. the expected number of users, sites, customers, samples, or annual analyses, as applicable;
6. requested territory and term;
7. the MetaSpici version, release tag, or commit to be licensed;
8. whether support, maintenance, validation, training, or custom development is requested;
9. whether affiliates, contractors, service providers, or customers need rights; and
10. any requested rights relating to the patents listed in `PATENTS.md`.

The Technology Transfer Center may request additional technical, compliance, or business information before issuing proposed terms.

## Process

1. The licensor reviews the proposed use and determines the required copyright, patent, support, and distribution scope.
2. If the request is acceptable, the licensor provides a proposal or draft agreement, including fees and conditions where applicable.
3. Rights begin only after all required parties sign the definitive agreement and any required fees are paid.

Submitting a request, receiving a quotation, or discussing proposed terms does not itself grant any right or waive any restriction in `LICENSE`.

The PolyForm license expressly addresses certain noncommercial organizations and purposes. Review the complete license before applying. If a collaboration includes commercial participants, productization, service delivery, or anticipated commercial application, request a written determination rather than assuming that the noncommercial license applies.
